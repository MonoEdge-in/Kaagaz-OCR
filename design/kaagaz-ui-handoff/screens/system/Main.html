v2Kaagaz by MonoEdge · Design system · 20 Oct 2026

Signal on paper.

The MonoEdge look, built for documents: warm off-white paper, near-black ink, one royal blue. Soft rounded surfaces instead of boxes, pill controls with mono labels, and a dotted signal texture used sparingly.

  1. 01Paper is the subjectPages look like pages: thumbnails, shadows, a real viewer. Chrome stays out of the way.
  2. 02Nothing guessedEvery value is Verified or Needs review. Every answer points at its spot on the page.
  3. 03Soft, not boxyWhite surfaces on warm paper, 20px corners, hairlines inside. Borders only where a shape means something (dashed = needs review).
  4. 04Dense to work, generous to read13px tables and lists. 16px answers and document text, with room for matras.

04 · Meaning

Status and sensitivity mean the same thing everywhere

Value and answer status

colour + icon + text + edge
VerifiedTwo readers agreed. Solid stamp with a check.
Needs reviewReaders disagreed or were unsure. Dashed amber edge.
From documentsAn answer built from cited pages, with at least one value not yet verified.
Not foundStated plainly, never a guess. Neutral grey.
Reading 4 of 6Calm motion: breathing dots and a slow underline. Stops for reduced motion.
Couldn’t readAlways with the reason and the fix: “Glare on page 3. Upload a sharper scan.”

Sensitivity labels

four fixed chips, each with its own shape
PublicFine to share outside. Brochures, published rates. Round marker.
InternalThe default. Everyday papers, GST invoices. Square marker.
ConfidentialContracts, bank statements, salary sheets, land records. Diamond marker.
RestrictedLand titles, board papers, ID documents. Triangle marker and an outline.
